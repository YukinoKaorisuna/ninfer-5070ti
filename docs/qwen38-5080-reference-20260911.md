## Why Q4 MTP KV

Q2 MTP KV caused a major speculative-quality regression.

At 98,304 context / 53,912-token prompt / chunk 1024:

Q2 MTP KV, MTP3:
- decode: 51.47 tok/s
- acceptance: 19.88%
- acceptance length: 1.60 tok/round
- accepted by position: 29,5,0

Q4 MTP KV, MTP3:
- decode: 74.52 tok/s
- acceptance: 45.83%
- acceptance length: 2.38 tok/round
- accepted by position: 12,7,3

Therefore Q2 MTP KV is not used in the reference configuration.

## Prefill chunk sweep

Dense sweep from 896 through 1280 in increments of 32:

- 896: 1452.99 tok/s, runtime reservation ~2.21 GiB
- 1024: 1434.78 tok/s, ~2.23 GiB
- 1248: 1441.45 tok/s, ~2.27 GiB
- 1280: 1471.32 tok/s, ~2.27 GiB

896 achieves approximately 98.75% of the measured 1280 peak while
using less runtime memory.

Chosen production-oriented chunk:

`896`

This is preferred over chasing the absolute prefill peak because
VRAM headroom is critical on the RTX 5080 16GB.
